Support

Something wrong, a card you'd argue with, a question? We read everything.

Something wrong with the game, a sentence you think is a mistake, or a rule you'd argue with? Write to us. We read everything, and card corrections go straight into the deck.

Email: errorslayer1@gmail.com

We usually answer within a few days. Please mention your device and iOS version, and the sentence word for word if it's about a card.

Common questions

Does it work offline?Yes. The app carries a built-in deck, and uses the full deck whenever it has a connection.
How do I reset my progress?Delete the app and reinstall it. Progress is stored only on your device, so that clears everything.
Why did a card come back?Cards you fail are reshuffled into the deck a little later. Cards you fix without a mistake are mastered and don't return.
I think a card is wrong.It happens; every sentence was written by a person. Send it to the email above with the word you'd change and why. If you're right, it's fixed for everyone.
What data do you collect?Practically none. The details are in the Privacy Policy, and it is short.
