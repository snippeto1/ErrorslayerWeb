Privacy Policy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The details, in plain English.

Effective date: 12 October 2026
Last updated: 12 October 2026

Error Slayer ("the App", "we", "us") is a grammar practice game published by Mikheil Gogilava ("the Developer"). This policy explains what information the App handles, what it does not handle, and the choices you have. We have written it to be read, not skimmed: it is short because the App does very little with your data.

The short version: Error Slayer has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress is stored on your own device. The only data that ever leaves your device is (1) the technical information your phone sends when it downloads a set of flashcards from our server, and (2) your scores and achievements if you choose to sign in to Apple's Game Center.

2. Information we do not collect

To avoid any doubt, the App does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in to anything we run;
  • collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, photos, contacts, location, or any other personal details;
  • use advertising, advertising identifiers (IDFA / AAID), or any advertising network;
  • sell anything inside the App or process any payment;
  • use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software of any kind;
  • share or sell any information to anyone for marketing purposes;
  • access your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, calendar or location; the App never asks for these permissions because it has no use for them;
  • track you across other apps or websites.

If a future version of the App ever changes any of the above, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent inside the App.

3. Information stored only on your device

The App keeps a small amount of information on your phone or tablet so that the game works:

  • your chosen difficulty level and whether sound is on or off;
  • whether you have completed the tutorial;
  • your best score;
  • which flashcards you have already mastered, so they are not shown to you again.

This information never leaves your device, is not visible to us, and is deleted when you delete the App. It is not linked to you as a person.

4. Information processed when the App downloads flashcards

The App's flashcards are stored on a server so that we can add and correct them without publishing a new version of the App. When the App fetches a set of cards, your device makes an ordinary internet request to that server, exactly as a web browser does when it opens a page. That request necessarily carries:

  • your device's IP address;
  • the time of the request;
  • basic technical details of the request (the App's version, the type of device and operating system).

This information is received by our hosting provider, Supabase, Inc. (see section 6), and is held in its standard server logs for security purposes, to detect abuse and keep the service running. We do not use it to identify you, build a profile of you, or contact you, and we do not combine it with any other data. Log entries are kept only for the period our hosting provider's standard logging allows, after which they are deleted.

The request does not include your scores, your progress, your settings or any other information from the App.

If you are offline, the App uses a built-in set of flashcards and makes no request at all.

The legal basis for this processing, where one is required, is our legitimate interest in operating and securing the service (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

5. Game Center (optional)

The App can show a leaderboard and achievements through Apple Game Center. This only happens if you are signed in to Game Center on your device; the App never asks you to create a Game Center account and works fully without one.

If you are signed in, the App sends your game score and the achievements you have earned to Game Center, and Game Center shows them to you and, according to your own Game Center settings, to your friends. Apple, not the Developer, operates Game Center, stores this data, and decides who can see it. The App receives from Apple only what it needs to display the leaderboard to you.

Apple's handling of Game Center data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ . You can sign out of Game Center, or restrict it, at any time in your device's Settings; the App will then stop sending scores.

7. International transfers

Our hosting provider, Supabase, stores the flashcard database and its server logs in the data-centre region selected for our project, which may be outside the country you live in. If so, the technical information in section 4 is transferred there. Supabase provides standard contractual safeguards for such transfers. Game Center data is handled by Apple according to Apple's own policies.

8. Children

The App is a general-audience educational game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children of any age; the App has no mechanism for anyone to submit personal information to us. Game Center for users under 13 is controlled by Apple's parental-control features, not by the App. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information in some way we have not foreseen, write to the address in section 1 and we will delete it.

9. Security

The App communicates with our server only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The App's connection to the database is read-only: it can download cards and cannot write, change or delete anything. Data on your device is protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID, encryption).

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security; but because the App holds almost no data about you, the consequences of any incident are correspondingly small.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act, or similar laws), you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict its processing;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

In practice, the only personal data we could hold is an IP address in a server log, which we cannot link back to you. Everything else is on your device: to delete it, delete the App. To exercise any right, write to the address in section 1; we may ask you for enough detail to locate the data concerned.

We do not sell personal information and have not done so in the preceding 12 months, in the meaning of the CCPA. We do not "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.
